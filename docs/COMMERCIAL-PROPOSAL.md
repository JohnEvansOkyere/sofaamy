## 1. Overview

Veloxa proposes to deliver the Sofaamy Business Management System as a production system —
covering design, quotation, fabrication, production tracking, and client communication — built to
Sofaamy's products, rules and workflow, and owned by Sofaamy.

The system has been demonstrated to Sofaamy's team, and the improvements requested at the
feedback session are included in this build.

## 2. Deliverables

| # | Module | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design configurator | Frame, Frameless and Curtain Wall product families; product-scoped design libraries; all of a client's windows and doors in one project workspace; 2D drawings, 3D and in-wall views |
| 2 | Quotation | Live GHS pricing from the design; discount, GETFund + NHIL, VAT and deposit terms; branded client quotation PDFs; unified quotation per project |
| 3 | Fabrication documents | Material lists generated automatically from the design using Sofaamy's confirmed rules; cutting lists with piece drawings, cut angles and per-window breakdown; cutting optimisation with waste reporting; glass and hardware schedules; work orders |
| 4 | Project management | Project assignment to staff with start/end dates; planning calendar; pipeline board covering every project from enquiry to delivery |
| 5 | Production & QA | Factory stages with sign-off, payment and QA gates, rework tracking |
| 6 | Dispatch & delivery | Delivery notes, vehicle and driver assignment, installation handover |
| 7 | Accounts | Invoicing from accepted quotations, deposit and balance tracking per project, payments ledger |
| 8 | Client communication | WhatsApp quotations and updates; shareable client design links |
| 9 | Users & roles | Role-based access and dashboards per department; audit trail |
| 10 | Hosting & data | Secure cloud hosting, daily backups; Sofaamy's catalogue, prices and staff set up |

Every calculation is validated against completed Sofaamy jobs before acceptance. Training is
provided for each department, followed by a parallel-run pilot before go-live.
