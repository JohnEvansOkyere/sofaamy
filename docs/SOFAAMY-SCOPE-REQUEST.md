## Covering Note — For Management

Following the system demonstration and Sofaamy's feedback, Veloxa was asked to submit a proposal
for the full production build. This document is the step immediately before that proposal.

A proposal is only as accurate as the requirement it answers. Sofaamy has offered to consolidate
input from every department, and this framework is the structure for that exercise. It is not a
description of what Veloxa thinks Sofaamy needs — it is a set of forms for each department to state,
in their own words, what the system must do for them.

**We are deliberately not writing Sofaamy's scope.** Sofaamy's team has decades of operating
knowledge in glass and aluminium fabrication that no software vendor can substitute for. What we can
do is make sure that when nine departments answer, the answers are complete, comparable, and
sufficient to price against.

### How this works

1. Sofaamy names one responsible person per department (page 2).
2. Each department completes its own form — only its own. Every form is 2 pages.
3. Sofaamy consolidates the completed forms and the supporting documents.
4. Veloxa reviews, and returns with any clarification questions in one batch.
5. **Veloxa submits the full proposal**, including scope, timeline, and pricing.

Step 5 depends entirely on step 3. Once the completed forms are in our hands, the proposal follows
without further delay.

### One request that matters more than the forms

**Send documents, not descriptions.** One completed job file — quotation, material calculation,
drawing, cutting list, work order, delivery note — tells us more than any written answer. A
photograph of a supplier invoice settles a price permanently. Confidential client names and margins
may be blacked out; please keep dimensions, calculations, document references, revisions and
workflow steps readable.

### A note on existing tools

We are not proposing to remove AutoCAD, SmartGlazier, or the Excel workbooks the team depends on.
Please answer on the assumption that the tools your department trusts stay. What we need to
understand is where each one fits and what must pass between them.
