# Sofaamy Workflow Discovery — Key Questions

**Prepared for:** Sofaamy Co. Ltd
**Prepared by:** Veloxa Technology Limited

## Purpose

We already understand that Sofaamy receives work through client enquiries, walk-ins, client measurements, and Sofaamy site visits. We also understand that measurements may be preliminary or final, quotations are prepared by the office/supervisor, and the workflow involves AutoCAD, SmartGlazier, Excel/manual calculations, procurement, production, QA, and installation.

This short questionnaire focuses only on the areas that still need confirmation before designing the unified workflow.

Please answer briefly and share actual examples where possible.

---

## 1. AutoCAD's place in the workflow

1. Which products or projects require AutoCAD?

2. Is AutoCAD normally used before the quotation, after the quotation, after client approval, or at more than one stage?

3. What information does the AutoCAD team receive to begin work?

4. What does the AutoCAD team return to the office, factory, or installer? For example: DWG, PDF, DXF, printed drawing, or cutting information.

5. Who reviews and approves the AutoCAD drawing before production?

6. How does the team identify the latest approved drawing revision?

7. What should happen when the AutoCAD drawing changes the dimensions, layout, materials, or price?

---

## 2. Quotation and design relationship

8. Can the office prepare a quotation before the AutoCAD drawing is complete?

9. Which products can be quoted from measurements and product selections alone?

10. Which products require AutoCAD or another detailed drawing before the final quotation?

11. Is the first quotation considered budgetary, final, or dependent on final measurement/drawing approval?

12. Who approves the final price, discounts, payment terms, and design before the job moves forward?

## 3. Excel and other calculation tools

13. Which Excel workbooks or other calculation tools are essential to preparing a quote, material list, or cutting list?

14. What does each important workbook calculate?

16. Who is responsible for updating prices, formulas, and product rules?

17. Which Excel outputs must be preserved or reproduced in the future system?
